# V37.5.8 — Business-card Start Page

Added a dedicated `/start` landing page for the business-card QR.

Purpose: give a new contact one useful front door to Pharma Service without forcing them straight into a contact form.

Primary paths:
- Browse the institutional catalogue.
- Send an RFQ / product list / requirement.
- Read how Pharma Service handles institutional supply.
- Open The Workshop for practical product guidance.

The page includes a compact enquiry form that reuses the existing safe public enquiry workflow and private RFQ storage. Enquiries submitted from this page are tagged `business_card_start_v37_5_8` in `source_page`.

Recommended QR URL: `https://pharmaservice.ae/start?ref=businesscard`

No portal, quote, order, demo, catalogue, Workshop, or Supabase schema behaviour was changed.
