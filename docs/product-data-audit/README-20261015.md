## Product experience classification is provisional

The master has **proposed** experiences, *not established legal classifications*. Classification from name, family and ID pattern is a work-routing heuristic only. The remaining items stay **Unclassified — review**, rather than being silently guessed. Especially **vitamin, herbal, topical, and device/kit** products may belong to different UAE regulatory routes and need explicit assessment. Never publish a pharmacological classification from a name heuristic.

## Minimum content standard by product experience

| Experience | Product identity needed first | Additional evidence to collect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pharmaceuticals | Exact name, active ingredients, strength, dosage form, route when relevant, exact pack | Manufacturer; UAE-authorized pack/registration and licensing route as applicable; locally appropriate product leaflet; dispensing precautions only from source; legal supply status |
| Supplements & wellness | Exact composition per serving, serving size, form, pack/net content, maker | Verified product category/registration, approved claims, cautions, storage and maker leaflet |
| Disposables & consumables | Material, sterile/nonsterile, size/dimensions, single-use status if relevant, pieces/box, sell unit | Relevant standards, expiry and compatibility, LATEX if relevant, packaging details |
| Devices & diagnostics | Make and exact model, measurement/function, in-box accessories | Accuracy/range/cuff/probe/strip compatibility, warranties, power, technical documentation |
| Capital equipment & infrastructure | Make/model or formal configurable requirement, dimensions, capacity and installation scope | Technical datasheet, accessories, delivery, warranty, training/maintenance and regulatory evidence where applicable |
| Sourcing requirements | Exact original formal wording, required/conditional/alternative status, intended commercial spec | A concrete source-confirmed brand/model must be selected before customer-facing exact SKU treatment |

**Data provenance:** distinguish `SOURCE_VERIFIED` (manufacturer/regulator datasheet/official leaflet), `SUPPLIER_WRITTEN` (supplier quote and exact pack), `PUBLIC_MARKET_BENCHMARK` (dated UAE retailer), `PSC_PLANNING` (planning only), `UNVERIFIED`, and `NOT_APPLICABLE`. Each validated field needs its own evidence URL/file, date and reviewer. Do not treat a missing *public projection* field as proof it is nowhere in the system.

## Recommended content-first enrichment workflow

1. **Identity crosswalk:** For each `source_kind=product`, cross-check existing SKU, family, chosen exact option ID, name, brand, strength/model, form and pack. Resolve collisions manually rather than fuzzily merging.
2. **Legal/product experience classification:** Decide medicines vs supplements vs consumables vs devices vs equipment using authoritative product registration and official manufacturer materials; mark unknowns as REVIEW.
3. **Evidence pack per SKU:** Manufacturer official product specification/leaflet and independent exact-pack label/photo; capture primary URLs, check date and evidence level. Supplier written confirmation needed for actual UAE stocking/cost.
4. **Controlled category fields:** Fill only grounded structured information. Do not model-invent indications, contraindications, dosages, ingredient quantities, manufacturer, registration, certification, specs or compatibility.
5. **Separate release gates:** Product identity; clinical/professional data; images; commercial price/stock; public release all independent. Never equate visual or historic benchmark evidence to commercial approval.
6. **Synchronization:** Once a record is approved for publication in a later task, update the controlled PSC workbook and limited website projection together with same revision, then run family/variant/search/request tests. No such update occurred in this audit.
7. **Only after content approval** should page theme/UX work begin. Continue preserving the current functional shells and request flow until explicit deployment authorization.
