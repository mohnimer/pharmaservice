The Zinc Problem Nobody Talks About

Published in the British Dental Journal, Neurology, and the American Journal of Medical Sciences

01

Daily Use

Zinc from adhesive is swallowed in small amounts every day with food and saliva

02

Zinc Builds Up

Excess zinc accumulates in the body over months and years of daily use

03

Copper Drops

Zinc and copper exist in equilibrium — as zinc rises, copper falls dangerously low

04

Nerve Damage

Copper deficiency causes anaemia, numbness, difficulty walking — potentially irreversible

GlaxoSmithKline voluntarily stopped manufacturing their zinc-containing Super Poligrip products after increased adverse event reports. Their own guidance: switch to a zinc-free alternative. Multiple peer-reviewed studies have documented cases ranging from neurological damage to one fatal outcome.
