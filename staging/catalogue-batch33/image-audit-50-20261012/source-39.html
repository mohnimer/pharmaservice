Kool Temp Cooling Sheet for children provides gentle and effective cooling relief for fever, headaches, or heat discomfort. Specially designed for kids’ sensitive skin, each sheet adheres comfortably to the forehead or body and delivers up to 8 hours of continuous cooling. Ready to use straight from the pack, these sheets are safe, mess-free, and ideal for home, travel, or school emergencies.

Overview

Cooling sheets specially designed for children Provides soothing relief from fever, headache, or heat stress Up to 8 hours of gentle cooling effect Safe for children’s sensitive skin Gentle adhesive keeps sheet securely in place without irritation Ready to use, no refrigeration needed Pack contains 6 individually wrapped sheets

How to use

Open the pouch and remove the sheet. Peel off the protective film and apply the gel side to the child’s forehead or affected area. Replace after up to 8 hours or when cooling effect wears off. Reseal unused sheets in pouch to maintain freshness.

Ingredients

Hydrogel Technology: Ensures sustained cooling effect Gentle, Skin-Safe Adhesive: Designed for delicate child skin Soft Backing Material: Provides comfort during use

Warning / Cautions

For external use only. Do not use on wounds, broken, or irritated skin. Discontinue use if redness, rash, or irritation occurs. Not a substitute for medical treatment in cases of high or persistent fever—consult a doctor. Use under adult supervision at all times. Keep out of reach of children when not in use.
