PSC V41 — STRONG PUBLIC ART-DIRECTION / DE-AI PASS

WHY THIS EXISTS
V40 was intentionally conservative and therefore did not produce enough visible change.
V41 is deliberately more decisive. It changes composition, hierarchy, surface language and motion — not just border radii.

VISIBLE CHANGES

HOME
- Editorial intro has much more whitespace and stronger asymmetry.
- Procurement details are now a horizontal technical ledger, not four cards.
- Clinical categories become a deliberately uneven editorial mosaic.
- Workshop preview becomes one feature story + two smaller stories.
- Account / portal teaser becomes a full-width dark punctuation band.

OUR MODEL
- Stronger editorial hero proportions.
- Source-per-line principle stops looking like a badge.
- Illustration frame and cards are flatter and sharper.
- Opening / recurring supply becomes a full split spread rather than two SaaS cards.
- Catalogue-flow explanation becomes a technical plate with shared rules instead of floating rounded cards.

START
- Route choices become a clean navigation ledger with stronger spacing and numbering.

CONTACT
- Form becomes square/precise rather than soft SaaS.
- Contact details become a ruled ledger.

WORKSHOP
- Guide landing becomes magazine-like: one lead article with two supporting articles.
- Decorative circles and generic hover lifts are removed.

CATALOGUE
- Functional cards remain because they are useful here.
- Corners are tighter; hover feedback is border/arrow based, not lift/shadow.
- Product behavior/data are unchanged.

HEADER
- Smaller logo / quieter nav.
- Active page uses an underline instead of a rounded white pill.

MOTION
- No universal "everything fades up".
- Copy, media and ledger/list content use three different motion families.
- Movement is 7–14px maximum and 0.7–1.25 seconds.
- Community animation is substantially slower.
- No decorative V41 motion is applied to portal/* or admin/*.
- prefers-reduced-motion is respected.

IMPORTANT
- Replaces the V40 public design stylesheet/script in index.html.
- Does NOT change app.js.
- Does NOT change styles.css.
- Does NOT change Supabase/backend/catalogue data/request/quotation logic.
- Keeps the V39.32 JS for useful loading and confirmation feedback.
- Keeps prior Start cleanup, community section, copy tweak and eyebrow removal.
